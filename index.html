✦ The studio

Six humans.
One obsession.

We're Pebble Studio — six designers, strategists and one feral illustrator working out of Delhi NCR. We make brands. The whole brand. From the late-night strategy doc to the tiny Instagram template the intern uses on a Sunday.

We don't do trends-for-trends'-sake. We don't do "premium minimal" for businesses that aren't either. We figure out what your brand actually sounds like and then we make every pixel sound like that.

Small projects, big projects, projects that started small and got embarrassingly big. We're up for it. Hi.

✦ What we make

A studio that
actually ships.

Six things we do well. Sometimes one of them. Sometimes all six at once. (Yes, we sleep.)

Identity that
actually means something.

Logo, type system, palette, voice, the whole worldview. We don't hand you a PDF and run — we build identities you can extend on a Tuesday afternoon when the social calendar needs filling. Logo + guidelines + assets + the rationale behind every choice.

Graphics & collateral

Pitch decks, posters, menus, lookbooks, packaging, presentations that don't make people scroll TikTok mid-meeting. Beautiful, on-brand, fast.

Brand strategy

Positioning, audience, brand architecture, messaging frameworks. We figure out what your brand stands for before we figure out what it looks like.

Social media & content

Templates, monthly content systems, campaign rollouts. We design the system and the seventeen Tuesday posts. Your team can run it after.

Illustration

One feral illustrator on staff. Editorial spots, brand mascots, packaging artwork, and the occasional 4-meter mural. Hand-drawn, weird, on-brief.

Campaigns

Launches, product drops, festival pushes. Concept → key art → motion → social cutdowns → OOH. End-to-end with one team that talks to itself.

Content & Research

Creative writing, deep-dive historical/market research, and sharp copywriting. We craft words that don't just fill space, but actually say something meaningful.

Web Design & UI

Clean, conversion-focused landing pages and portfolio sites. We design digital spaces that feel like a natural extension of your entire brand's worldview.

✦ How we work

Four phases.
No nonsense.

A typical project runs 6–10 weeks. Sprints can be faster. Bigger systems take longer. We'll be honest about it on the call.

01

Listen

A 90-minute kickoff. We ask the awkward questions. You answer them. We take a lot of notes.

02

Strategise

Positioning doc, audience, voice, brand architecture. This is where the brand actually starts to exist.

03

Make

Two design directions. Real applications, not Pinterest moodboards. You pick. We push the chosen one further.

04

Ship

Final files, guidelines, hand-off, a short Loom walkthrough. We stick around for the first launch.
